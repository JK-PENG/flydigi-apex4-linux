## First actual OW2 bounded dry-run

After explicit confirmation, OW2 started with the bounded dry-run launch option.
The game process, one ordinary DualSense and the dry-run safety banner were all
observed. The holder completed three full Hanzo charges.

The first two resistance cycles were accepted about seven seconds apart. Each
produced R2 mode 1 `(60,40)` and a Normal one second later. The original
ten-second total window then expired and forced another Normal; the third human
action was rejected. There was no physical trigger write or write failure.
After the user exited the game, the wrapper and game disappeared and one Edge
with one installed relay was restored.

Result: cycle limiting and resets PASS, but the ten-second interaction window
does not fit three normal user-paced actions. The default was changed to thirty
seconds while retaining at most three one-second active cycles, so maximum
active output remains three seconds. The corrected window requires another OW2
dry-run before physical testing.
